How this tool works

Starting fresh? Fill in the details below, then in Step 2 upload your two data sources (e.g. Tally ledger + Bank statement) as Source A and Source B.

Continuing a reconciliation you already started? Use the file you got back last time (the one with "Unmatched - Tally Only" and "Unmatched - Bank Only" sheets) - upload that same file twice in Step 2, once as Source A (pick the "Unmatched - Tally Only" sheet) and once as Source B (pick the "Unmatched - Bank Only" sheet). It carries your leftover unmatched transactions straight through so you can keep clearing them.

Reconciling GSTR-2B against your Purchase Register? Set the Reference column to Invoice Number (or GSTIN+Invoice No combined, if your file has that) on both sides in Step 2 - invoices are matched by that reference first, regardless of date, since GSTR-2B dates and your book dates commonly differ by weeks or months. Set Date tolerance wide (e.g. 60+ days) to also catch any date-based matches for rows with no usable reference.

What the "Past Reconciliation Summary" upload below is - and isn't: it's a small running log of past reconciliation totals (date, client, match rates) for your own reference - it is not where you continue an in-progress reconciliation. Leave it blank unless you specifically want to see past summaries for this client.
